Execution Protocol

4 PHASES
01

Grip & Setup

Grasp the lat bar with an overhand grip slightly wider than shoulder width. Lock thighs tightly beneath the contoured roller pads so feet remain planted firmly.

02

The Drive

Depress and retract scapulae downward before bending arms. Lead movement by driving elbows toward the ribs until the bar gently reaches the upper clavicle.

03

Peak Contraction

Hold and violently squeeze the lat musculature for 1 second. Keep the chest proud and avoid excessive extension or arching in the lumbar spine.

04

Controlled Eccentric

Resist the load steadily through a controlled 2-3 second return tempo until arms reach complete upward extension and lats are deeply stretched.

warning

Technical Faults to Avoid

Caution
cancel
Excessive backward swing

Using torso momentum to heave the load turns the movement into a low row and diffuses lat tension.

cancel
Pulling behind the neck

Severely compromises the glenohumeral joint and creates destructive rotator cuff impingement forces.

cancel
Truncating overhead stretch

Halting the ascent early skips the lengthened-state muscle hypertrophy where high tension occurs.
