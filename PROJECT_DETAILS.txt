STATIONX — FULL PROJECT DETAILS
================================

1. WHAT IT IS
-------------
StationX is a local-first strength and cardio training log for phones, built with
Flutter (Dart). You log every set, rep and weight of your workouts, follow a fixed
rotation of workouts (for example Chest + Biceps -> Back + Triceps -> Legs + Shoulders),
and the app calculates volume, estimated one-rep max (1RM), progression suggestions and
personal records (PRs) for you. Cardio (runs, treadmill, bike, etc.) is tracked too.

Everything works offline. Your data lives in a database on your device. An optional cloud
backup/sync (Supabase) and optional read-only Health Connect / Apple Health sleep and
resting-heart-rate data can be turned on by the user; both are off by default.

  App name ........ StationX
  Version ......... 1.0.0+1
  Package / app id  dev.mahdi_ramadhan.stationx
  Platforms ....... Android (primary, min SDK 26), iOS/desktop scaffolding present
  Framework ....... Flutter 3.47 / Dart 3.13, Material 3
  Author .......... Mahdi Ramadhan


2. DESIGN
---------
The UI/UX was designed in Google Stitch (reference exports are kept in design_reference/)
and implemented as the "Kinetic Obsidian" dark theme: near-black canvas (#121416) with a
lime accent (#B8F000). Fonts are bundled (Space Grotesk, Geist, JetBrains Mono) so the app
makes no font requests. Design tokens live in lib/core/theme (colours, typography, spacing,
radius, motion) and shared widgets in lib/core/widgets (buttons, cards, chips, sheets,
charts, numeric keypad, muscle map, etc.). Screens never use raw colours; they use
context.sx and the Sx* tokens.

Stitch's mock-only content (sensors, mesocycles, social features) was replaced with honest
empty/"not available" states. Exercise "images" are vector muscle-map illustrations drawn in
code instead of photos.


3. FEATURES
-----------
Entry
  * Landing page, local account (create / log in), "Continue as Guest (Offline Mode)".
  * Optional cloud sign-in and "Restore from cloud backup" on a new phone.

Workout sections & limits
  * Workout preview, editor and the live logger group exercises into muscle sections (for example
    Legs, Shoulders) from the muscle metadata, keeping your exercise order.
  * Maximum workout length (Profile > Workout configuration: 2 h / 3 h / 4 h / Off, default 3 h,
    device-only). When it is reached the workout ends and saves automatically (only sets you marked
    done; duration capped; rotation advances once) - also if the app was closed meanwhile.
  * An in-progress workout survives the app being killed ("Resume workout" on Today).

Health apps (opt-in, off by default; Profile > Health & workouts)
  * Samsung Health works through Android Health Connect (Apple Health on iOS): save your cardio and
    strength sessions there, fill heart rate / calories from your watch (you confirm), and import
    workouts recorded elsewhere (preview first, never duplicated).

Schedule setup (after registering; optional for guests; also in Workouts and Profile)
  * Choose a program: Full Body, Upper / Lower, Push / Pull / Legs, Bro split, or Custom.
  * "Quick start" takes a preset as is: pick, answer "How many days a week?", review, save.
    "Customize exercises" lets you tick exercises per day and per muscle, grouped into
    practical sub-sections (for example Back -> Lats, Upper back & traps, Lower back), with
    a one-line reason per suggestion and sets/reps steppers.
  * "+ Add exercise" inside the flow creates your own exercise and ticks it immediately.
  * Custom: name your days, choose muscles per day; suggestions come from the recommender.
  * Days per week only sets the weekly session goal. The program is a SEQUENCE: if you miss
    a day you simply continue with the next workout.
  * Review shows sets and minutes per day plus a collapsible "Muscle coverage" summary and
    calm optional hints. Skip is available everywhere; leaving midway asks first.
  * Saving a new schedule archives the previous workouts (never deletes them) and keeps all
    history. Guests get a dismissible prompt on Today while they have no schedule or history.

Today (home)
  * Next workout from the rotation, with exercise/set/minute estimates, Start / Preview.
  * Weekly summary (sessions done, volume, records), recent progress, recovery summary
    from Health Connect / Apple Health when connected.

Workouts
  * Workout library and detail, create / edit / delete workouts, reorder exercises.
  * "Change next workout" (move the rotation pointer).
  * Replace / Smart Swap: ranks replacement exercises with the muscle-aware recommender
    (same muscle subgroup first, then movement, purpose, equipment, your history).
  * "Archived" section: workouts that are no longer in the rotation (for example after you
    change your schedule). They stay openable, editable and startable, can be added back to
    the rotation, and are only deleted if you delete them yourself.

Active workout (strength logger)
  * Per-exercise set rows (weight, reps, done), numeric keypad, previous performance,
    progression suggestion, rest timer, plate calculator, notes, finish / discard.
  * Finishing a workout advances the rotation; backdated sessions are supported.

Exercises
  * Library of 212 built-in exercises with search, muscle filter, sub-muscle filter (for
    example Back -> Lats / Upper back / Traps / Lower back), equipment filter and a
    "Common" quick filter.
  * Exercise details: muscle map, primary and assisting muscles (from the muscle profile),
    history, PRs, e1RM chart. Custom exercises can be created.

Progress
  * Volume and strength trends, estimated 1RM chart, personal records, muscle-group
    breakdown, improvement summaries.

History
  * Session list, calendar view, session details, edit and delete.

Cardio
  * 20 activity types (treadmill, outdoor/trail run, hiking, bikes incl. spin and air bike, rowing,
    ski erg, elliptical, arc trainer, stair/vertical climber, swimming, arm ergometer, HIIT, boxing,
    jump rope, custom); rowing/ski erg show pace per 500 m, swimming per 100 m.
  * Live cardio session and manual logging for run / treadmill / bike / row / etc.
    Fields adapt to the activity type (distance, duration, pace, incline, resistance, heart
    rate, calories...). Cardio goals, records, history, edit and delete.

Profile
  * Name, body metrics, weight unit (kg / lb), theme (including OLED), workout config.
  * Integrations & storage: Health Connect / Apple Health, cloud backup & sync, JSON/CSV
    export (to clipboard), Import from Gym Tracker (see section 9).
  * App & safety: privacy policy page, delete all local data, version label.


4. PRODUCT RULES (do not break)
-------------------------------
  * Workout rotation is index-based, not calendar-based. It advances only when a workout
    is completed.
  * "workout_date" (when you trained; can be backdated) is always separate from
    "created_at" (when the row was entered).
  * Progression, 1RM (Epley), volume and PR calculations live in the pure-Dart domain
    layer. UI widgets never calculate them.
  * Cardio fields adapt by activity type.
  * Local-first: the app is fully usable offline; the network is optional.
  * No fake data where real data exists. Seed/demo data is only shown for guests/demo mode
    and is marked "clean" so it never syncs as if the user created it.


5. ARCHITECTURE
---------------
State management: plain Flutter (ChangeNotifier + ListenableBuilder + an AppScope
InheritedWidget). No state-management package. Navigation goes through one AppNav helper.

lib/
  app/        AppController, bootstrap (opens Isar, health, cloud), app shell, navigation
  core/       theme tokens, shared widgets, formatters
  domain/     PURE DART: models (Exercise, Workout, WorkoutSession, CardioSession, profile,
              records...), SyncMeta {createdAt, updatedAt, syncStatus}, repository
              interfaces, services (RotationService, ProgressionService, PrService,
              VolumeService, SmartSwapService, PlateCalculator, WorkoutCompletion,
              cardio metrics, health summary, formulas)
  data/
    memory/   in-memory repositories (tests, demo)
    isar/     Isar persistence: entities, mappers, write-through-cache repositories
    seed/     exercise catalog, default workouts, demo history
    health/   Health Connect / HealthKit gateway (read-only sleep + resting heart rate),
              consent store
    sync/     Supabase sync: config guard, auth, gateway, SyncEngine, controller
    import/   Gym Tracker importer (plan -> apply), exercise alias map, file source
  features/   one folder per area: landing, auth, today, workouts, active_workout,
              exercises, progress, history, cardio, profile, cloud, health, shell,
              onboarding (schedule setup flow)

The UI depends only on repository interfaces and domain services, so storage can change
without touching screens.

Muscle and recommendation layer (all pure Dart, in lib/domain):
  models/muscle.dart ........ taxonomy: MuscleRegion (11) -> Muscle (practical subdivisions),
                              MuscleTarget (region, optional subgroup, primary/assisting role,
                              optional emphasis/weight), ExerciseMuscleProfile (many targets),
                              MuscleWeights (primary 1.0, assisting 0.5 - one place)
  services/muscle_profiles .. detailed profile for each of the 212 built-in exercises; broad
                              fallback derived from the stored fields when nothing else exists
  services/muscle_coverage .. coverage of a workout, a program or logged history: direct sets
                              (primary only) vs weighted sets (plus assisting), High /
                              Moderate / Low levels per week (practical guideline numbers)
  services/exercise_recommender  one engine for day generation, replacement ranking and
                              informational gap hints; SmartSwapService delegates to it
  services/split_catalog .... presets, sub-sections, "common" list, suggestions
  services/split_models + schedule_builder .. the designed days and saving them as workouts
                              and a rotation (never deletes, can restore archived workouts)
The simplified 7-way MuscleGroup stays as the broad layer used by storage, sync and simple
UI, so the detailed taxonomy needed no schema change for built-in exercises.


6. DATA & PERSISTENCE
---------------------
  * Isar (isar_community 3.3.2, the maintained fork) is the on-device database, behind a
    DataStore abstraction. Repositories keep an in-memory cache and write through to Isar.
  * Every record carries SyncMeta (createdAt, updatedAt, syncStatus) so sync is possible
    without changing the data model.
  * Batch writes (for example imports) use a single transaction and a single notification.
  * Opening the app tops up the exercise catalog: seed exercises that are missing are added;
    existing and custom exercises are never overwritten or deleted.


6b. MUSCLE TAXONOMY & RECOMMENDATIONS
-------------------------------------
  * Taxonomy: Chest (upper / mid / lower), Back (lats, upper back, traps, lower back),
    Shoulders (front / side / rear delts), Biceps (long head, short head, brachialis),
    Triceps (long / lateral / medial head), Forearms, Quadriceps, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves,
    Core (abs, obliques, deep core). It is practical gym terminology, not an anatomy claim.
  * Every built-in exercise has structured targets: primary and assisting muscles, with a
    subgroup only where it is defensible; otherwise the whole region (no fake precision).
    Examples: Lat Pulldown = Back -> Lats (assisting: biceps, upper back); Incline DB Press =
    Chest -> Upper chest (assisting: triceps, front delts); Romanian Deadlift = hamstrings
    (assisting: glutes, lower back).
  * Primary vs assisting is never treated the same: a primary set counts 1.0, an assisting set
    0.5. "Direct" coverage counts only primary work, so Bench Press is direct chest work and
    only assisting work for triceps and front delts.
  * Used for: schedule/day generation (a day stops adding exercises to a muscle once it is
    covered), replacement ranking, muscle coverage (workout, program, history), weekly volume
    levels and informational "might be under-trained" hints. Hints are optional suggestions,
    never commands.
  * Equipment filtering is supported by the engine (allowedEquipment) but there is no
    equipment question in onboarding yet (planned for later).
  * Custom exercises use the SAME structure as built-ins: region, optional subgroup, role
    (primary / assisting) and optional emphasis, stored in a new nullable field (Isar
    muscleTargetsJson, Supabase column muscle_targets jsonb). The create-exercise sheet has a
    "Muscles trained" editor. The same 1.0 / 0.5 weighting and recommender apply. Exercises
    created before this change keep working with broad, region-level targets.
    IMPORTANT: apply supabase/migrations/20261009000000_exercise_muscle_targets.sql to the
    Supabase project BEFORE shipping this build; without the column, exercise sync shows the
    normal sync error and retries (nothing is lost).

7. OPTIONAL CLOUD SYNC (SUPABASE)
---------------------------------
  * Off unless the build is configured with a Supabase URL + PUBLISHABLE (anon) key and the
    user signs in. The config guard refuses secret keys (sbp_..., sb_secret_..., service_role).
  * Backend (supabase/migrations/20261008000000_initial_schema.sql): per-user tables with
    composite primary key (user_id, id), Row Level Security (owner-only), last-write-wins
    trigger, server_updated_at pull cursor, tombstones (deleted_at) for deletions and a
    delete_account() RPC that removes the account and all its data.
  * Client SyncEngine: push -> tombstones -> pull; idempotent and safe to repeat. The
    CloudSyncController adds debounce, retry, single-flight and resume throttling, and
    handles the "signed into a different account" decision (merge or replace).
  * Config lives in env/supabase.json (git-ignored; template env/supabase.example.json).
    Only the publishable key may ever be in the app. See supabase/README.md.


8. HEALTH INTEGRATION
---------------------
Read-only sleep and resting heart rate from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS)
through the "health" package. Strictly opt-in: the app explains what is read before asking
for permission, never writes to the health store, and the data stays on the device.


9. IMPORT FROM GYM TRACKER (ONE-TIME MIGRATION)
-----------------------------------------------
StationX replaces an older personal app, Gym Tracker (../gym_tracker). Gym Tracker got a new
Settings > Data > "Export Data" button that writes a versioned JSON file
("gym_tracker_export" v1; spec in gym_tracker/docs/EXPORT_FORMAT.md). In StationX:
Profile > Integrations > "Import from Gym Tracker" -> choose the file (or paste copied data)
-> review a preview -> Import.

  * Only completed workouts are imported; each keeps the date you trained.
  * Importing the same file twice adds nothing (ids are gt_s<id>).
  * Existing StationX data is never modified or deleted.
  * Exercises are matched by name (exact, then a curated alias list); unknown ones become
    custom exercises with an inferred muscle group and equipment (shown in the preview).
  * PRs and estimated 1RM are recomputed from the imported history; achievements are ignored.
  * Optional checkbox: make the next workout match where Gym Tracker left off.
  * Weights are kg on both sides (no unit conversion). Malformed entries are skipped one by
    one; files over 50 MB are refused.
  * Code: lib/data/import/ and lib/features/profile/gym_tracker_import_flow.dart.


10. SECURITY & PRIVACY
----------------------
  * No analytics, no ads, no tracking; no network access unless the user turns on cloud sync.
  * Passwords are validated by forms only and never stored or logged; cloud auth uses
    Supabase Auth.
  * RLS makes every cloud row owner-only; verified against the real Supabase project by an
    opt-in end-to-end test (including attempts to read/modify another user's rows).
  * Secrets are never committed; env/supabase.json is git-ignored.
  * Privacy policy: docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md (also shown in the app).
