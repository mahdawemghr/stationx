Delete your StationX account and data

A StationX account consists of a username and a password only (no e-mail address is collected). You can delete it, and all data stored in it, yourself.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open StationX and log in (if you are not logged in already).
  2. Go to Profile and scroll to the account section.
  3. Tap Delete account. (To keep the account but remove your training data, tap Delete all my data instead.)
  4. Read the warning and confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your login: the username and the password hash.
  • Every row stored on the server for your account: profile and settings, training schedule, workout and cardio history, goals, custom exercises and activities.

Nothing is kept afterwards for that account.

What stays on your phone

Your workouts and history live only in your account, so deleting the account removes them. The phone keeps just the temporary workout draft, which is removed when you delete the account or log out. StationX is not backed up by Android. Records StationX saved to Health Connect / Apple Health stay there until you delete them in that app.
